Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how AVENOR handles personal information when you visit our website, submit a project enquiry, contact us as a brand or apply to our creator network. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers or sensitive personal information through general enquiry forms.

1. Information we collect

  • General enquiries: name, email, phone, company/brand, division, project type, budget range and message.
  • Brand enquiries: company name, contact person's name, work email, phone/WhatsApp, website or social link, estimated budget and requirements.
  • Creator applications: name/stage name, email, phone, social platform, profile/handle, follower count, niche, optional media-kit link and audience information you provide.
  • Technical data: hosting/security logs may include IP address, browser/device details and request timestamps.
  • Preferences: the home page stores your light/dark theme choice in browser local storage.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, assess project requirements, contact prospective clients and creators, evaluate brand–creator opportunities, prepare proposals, coordinate requested services, maintain business records, protect the website and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

3. How information is shared

The current brand and creator forms send submitted details to our application backend. The backend is configured to store lead records in MongoDB and send enquiry notifications through Telegram. The website is hosted/delivered through Vercel and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical or submitted information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy practices. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, investigate misuse or handle a business reorganisation.

4. Cookies and local storage

The current home page stores the selected display theme in browser local storage. This is a local preference, not an advertising tracker. We do not currently state that the website uses analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting providers may use essential technical mechanisms for security and service delivery. If non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

5. Retention

We retain enquiry and application information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, assess a potential engagement, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. You may request deletion, subject to information that must be retained for legal, contractual, security or legitimate record-keeping reasons.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No website, database, messaging service or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send passwords, card details, identity numbers or other sensitive information through public forms or ordinary messages.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you submitted, and raise concerns about its handling. You may choose not to submit information, although we may then be unable to respond or consider your application. To make a request, use the contact form on the AVENOR website and identify the email address or phone number used in your submission. We may verify your request before acting.

8. Children and external links

Our business services and creator/brand enquiry flows are not intended for children. Do not submit a child's personal information. Our website may link to social networks or external services; we do not control their privacy practices, so please review their policies before submitting information.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our website, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version and date will be published here. For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, contact AVENOR through the contact form on our website.
